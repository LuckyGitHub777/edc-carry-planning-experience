Privacy & disclosures.

This document describes the prelaunch demo as packaged in the source repository. Review and replace it for the actual hosting provider and any enabled integrations before a public launch.

What the kit builder does

The kit builder calculates recommendations in your browser. Calculations do not submit mission, format or budget choices to an application backend. Sharing a kit link encodes these three choices in the URL; anyone with that URL can read them, and a hosting provider may log the requested URL. This prototype does not ask for your name or email and does not include third-party analytics scripts or checkout forms.

Hosting and external services

A web host may automatically process network data, including IP addresses and request logs, under its own policies. This description must be updated once a real host, analytics, email service, or checkout provider is connected. Digital purchases, if activated, occur on an external checkout service governed by that provider's terms and privacy policy.

Affiliate relationships

There are no active affiliate links in this prelaunch version. If compensated recommendations are introduced, we will disclose each relationship clearly near the relevant recommendation. We do not claim product testing we have not performed. Merchant prices and availability change and must be checked directly with sellers.

Editorial scope

Product classes and budget numbers are illustrative. Nothing on this site constitutes professional medical, financial, legal or aviation advice. Confirm restrictions and safety requirements independently.

Contact and policy readiness

A dedicated customer-support address and final legal entity are not yet configured; public commercial launch is blocked until contact information, accurate business identity, terms, and customer refund procedures are provided where applicable.
